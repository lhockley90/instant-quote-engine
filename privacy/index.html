Privacy notice

Last updated 24 October 2026

This notice explains how AiEngineered (a trading name of Liam Hockley, a sole trader in the United Kingdom) handles personal information. Contact: L.hockley17121990@gmail.com.

Businesses that buy a calculator

We keep your name, email address, business name, town and the details on your calculator, so we can set it up, send you requests and help you. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we don’t see your full card details. We keep this while you are a customer and for up to six years afterwards for our accounts.

People who send a request through a calculator

When you send a request, your name, phone number, email, the job you picked and any notes are emailed to the business whose calculator you used. That business decides how to use them, so please check their privacy information too. We keep a copy of each request email so we can check it was delivered and help if something goes wrong. Ask us to delete it at any time.

Visitors to this website

This website doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider keeps basic technical logs for security.

Who we share information with

Only the services we need to run AiEngineered: Stripe (payments), Vercel (hosting), n8n (sending requests) and Google (email). Some of these may process information outside the UK under appropriate safeguards.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing us. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
