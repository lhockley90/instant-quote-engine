About AiEngineered

AiEngineered is a small UK business run by Liam Hockley. It builds instant quote calculators for trades, clinics, salons and other local businesses.

The idea is simple. Most people want a rough price before they call anyone, and most small businesses don’t have time to answer “how much?” all day. A calculator answers it straight away and sends you the people who are happy with the price.

AiEngineered is new, so you won’t find a wall of reviews here yet. You can see exactly what you would get before paying anything: build a free preview with your business name and town.
