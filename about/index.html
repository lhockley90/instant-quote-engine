About AiEngineered

AiEngineered is a small UK business run by Liam Hockley. It builds instant quote calculators for trades, clinics, salons and other local businesses.

Most people want a rough price before they contact anyone, and most small businesses don’t have time to answer “how much?” all day. A calculator answers it straight away and sends you the people who are happy with the price.

New, and open about it

AiEngineered is a new business, so you won’t find a wall of reviews here yet. Instead, you can see exactly what you would get before paying anything: build a free preview with your business name and town.

How we work
