Straight answers for trades, clinics and local businesses about pricing online and getting more enquiries.

Should you show prices on your website?

Usually yes, as a guide range rather than a fixed price. It answers the first question most customers have, filters out people whose budget doesn’t fit, and you still confirm the final price.

How to add a quote calculator to your website

Paste one line of embed code into your website builder’s HTML or embed block, or share the calculator as a link in your Instagram bio, Facebook page and Google Business Profile. No website? The link works on its own.

Quote calculator or contact form?

A contact form asks customers to wait for a price; a quote calculator shows a guide price first and sends you the job, size and budget with the enquiry. Calculators suit businesses whose customers ask “how much?” first; contact forms suit work that can’t be priced without a conversation.
