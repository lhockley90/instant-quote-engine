Questions

What is an instant quote calculator?

A page where your customers pick a job and see a guide price straight away, instead of calling or messaging to ask. If the price suits them, they send you their name, phone, email and the job in one tap.

How much does it cost?

£197 to set up, then £49 a month. The monthly plan keeps your calculator online, sends every request to your inbox, and covers price changes and help. You can cancel any time.

Can I see it before I pay?

Yes. Build a free preview with your business name and town and you will see exactly what your customers would see. You only pay if you switch it on.

How does it get my prices right?

It starts from typical UK guide prices for your type of business, adjusted for your town. Clinics, salons and other local businesses change their own menu and prices from a private link. Trades tell us what they charge and we move the price level up or down to match, which is included in the monthly plan.

Do I need a website?

No. Put your link in your Instagram bio, Google Business Profile, Facebook page, WhatsApp or a QR code on your van or flyers. If you do have a website, it goes on with one line of code, or we install it for you free.

What happens when a customer sends a request?

You get an email with their name, phone number, email, the job, the guide price they saw and any notes, plus buttons to call or WhatsApp them in one tap. Pressing reply answers the customer directly.
