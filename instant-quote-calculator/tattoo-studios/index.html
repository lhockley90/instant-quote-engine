Questions from tattoo studios

Can clients tell me about placement and size?

Yes. The request form has a notes box for placement, size, style references and colour, and it all arrives in your email.

Can I change the styles and prices?

Yes, any time, from your private edit link.

How much does it cost?

£197 to set up, then £49 a month. The monthly plan keeps your calculator online, sends every request to your inbox, and covers price changes and help. You can cancel any time.

Do I need a website?

No. Put your link in your Instagram bio, Google Business Profile, Facebook page, WhatsApp or a QR code on your van or flyers. If you do have a website, it goes on with one line of code, or we install it for you free.

How do I cancel?

Use the “Manage my plan” link in your welcome email, which opens Stripe’s secure billing page. Your plan runs to the end of the month you have paid for, and then requests stop.
